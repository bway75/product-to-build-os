    60	## Three boundaries that cause most of the confusion
    61	
    62	**1. Infrastructure is Kade's wherever it surfaces.** A tool, gate, harness, service, or bus failure
    63	is infrastructure even when it breaks inside the PM cluster. It goes to Kade, not to that cluster's
    64	process lead. Athena owns PM practice, not the machinery either cluster runs on.
    65	
    66	**"Process" is three different things, so route by which one you mean.** Kade owns process
    67	IMPLEMENTATION, the machinery that enforces a process. **Ledger** owns process and governance
    68	DOCUMENTATION, the written rules and the catalog. **Athena** owns PM PRACTICE, how the planning
    69	cluster works. "Process changes" is not automatically Kade's: only building or running the thing that
    70	enforces it is.
    71	
    72	**2. Specifying an instrument does not make it yours.** Bob writes the spec for a measurement
    73	instrument or a gate; Kade builds, wires, owns, and reviews it. A measurement instrument is
    74	infrastructure, not analysis.
    75	
    76	**3. Nobody accepts their own work.** A gate's owner never treats its own gate as acceptance of that
    77	gate, or of the work the gate was built to judge. A green gate is evidence. Acceptance is the
    78	independent review, on record. This applies to Kade first, since Kade builds the gates.
